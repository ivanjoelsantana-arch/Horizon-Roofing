Worry 08
Will it actually hold up to our weather?

It is designed for this valley. On purpose.

The Okanagan is brutal on roofs: intense summer UV, heavy snow loads, wind-driven rain and constant freeze thaw cycles. A roof that works in Vancouver can fail here. So we design every roof as a complete system for local conditions, not just a new surface.

That means the right underlayment, proper ventilation, careful flashing at every transition and penetration, and where it makes sense, standing seam metal that resists UV, sheds snow efficiently and needs very little maintenance for decades.

  • Systems chosen for UV, snow and freeze thaw
  • Ventilation and moisture control done properly
  • Standing seam metal specialists
